Introduction to Tom & Jerry

Tom and Jerry is a famous cartoon made by William Hanna and Joseph Barbera


Tom: A house cat who tries hard to catch Jerry.
Jerry: A small mouse who outsmarts Tom every time.

Theme

. It focuses on the funny and chaotic fights between a blue-gray house cat named Tom and a smart brown mouse named Jerry.

Style of Humor

Slapstick Action: The show uses loud, funny physical comedy. Tom gets hit with pans, falls from high places, and gets zapped, but he always comes back for the next round.

No Talking: The characters rarely speak words. They use sounds, facial expressions, and great music to tell the story.

why people like tom and jerry?

Tom and Jerry is loved worldwide because its brilliant slapstick comedy, expressive animation, and near-total lack of dialogue create a universal language of fun that transcends language barriers and appeals to all ages.
